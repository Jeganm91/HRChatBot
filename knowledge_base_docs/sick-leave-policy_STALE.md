# India Sick Leave Policy
Effective: 2022-01-01 (never updated since)

Employees are entitled to 6 Sick Leave days per calendar year,
non-carry-forward. Medical certificate required beyond 2 consecutive
days of absence.
Note: Real policy changed to 10 days in 2024, but no updated
version of this document was ever created.
