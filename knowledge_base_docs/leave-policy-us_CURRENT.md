# US Leave Policy
Effective: 2025-01-01

Employees follow a unified PTO structure of 20 days per year,
inclusive of vacation and personal time. Sick leave is tracked
separately at 8 days per year. Unused PTO up to 5 days may be
carried forward to the next calendar year.
