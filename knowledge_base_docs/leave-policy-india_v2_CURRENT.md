# India Leave Policy (v2 - Revised)
Effective: 2025-01-01

Casual Leave entitlement is 15 days per calendar year, with up to 3
unused days allowed to carry forward into Q1 of the following year.
Sick Leave is 10 days per year.
This revision supersedes all prior versions of the India Leave Policy.
