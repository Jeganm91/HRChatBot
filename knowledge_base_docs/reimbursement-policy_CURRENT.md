# Expense Reimbursement Policy
Effective: 2025-01-01

Employees may claim reimbursement for business travel, client meals,
and approved training expenses. Claims must be submitted within 30
days with original receipts. Domestic per diem: INR 2500/day (India),
USD 75/day (US), AED 300/day (UAE).
